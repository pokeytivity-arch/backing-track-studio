Chord chart

Split bars with |. Chords in one bar share it equally. . holds the last chord for one slot, % repeats the bar, N.C. is no chord. Slash chords like A/C# and extensions like Gm11, C7alt, F7b9 work. Your own voicing: [C / E G C] is C in the left hand, E G C in the right.

Click a slot and type a chord. An empty slot holds the chord before it. Put a chord on an & (or e/a in 16ths) to push it ahead of the beat. Type a voicing like C / E G C (left hand / right hand) for exact notes. Enter moves to the next slot.
