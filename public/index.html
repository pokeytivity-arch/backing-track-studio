Chord chart

Split bars with |. Chords in one bar share it equally. . holds the last chord for one slot, % repeats the bar, N.C. is no chord. Slash chords like A/C# and extensions like Gm11, C7alt, F7b9 work. Your own voicing: [C / E G C] is C in the left hand, E G C in the right.

Recording

Choose an audio file or record with the microphone. Pokey finds the notes and drops them onto the keyboard in time with the recording. Works best with a clear piano part. It reads the first five minutes. The recording is sent to a piano transcription service to be analyzed and is not kept.
